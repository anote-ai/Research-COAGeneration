# Conference Papers

Papers are organized by research track first. Venue-specific manuscripts live
inside each track so the two paper projects do not blur together.

- `coa/coa-bench/dai2026/`: DAI Industry Track COA benchmark/system paper
- `coa/coa-bench/aaai2027/`: AAAI COA benchmark/evaluation paper
- `meta-routing/dai2026/`: MetaRoute-Bench framework, artifact, operational evaluation, and deployment lessons
- `meta-routing/aaai2027/`: learned budget-aware routing method and executable-task evaluation
- `oracle2026/`: multilingual and culturally grounded routing evaluation

Shared implementation belongs in `src/metarouter` or `src/coageneration`; paper
folders must not contain copied source trees. The papers may share
infrastructure and background citations, but each archival submission must have a
distinct research question, method, primary experiments, headline results, and
conclusion.
