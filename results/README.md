# Experiment Artifacts

Results are organized by paper track first.

- `coa/coa-bench/main/`: COA-Bench adversarial planning results.
- `meta-routing/dai2026/main/`: eight-policy DAI meta-routing experiment with
  180 generated task profiles, 30 paired seeds, and 43,200 traces.
- `meta-routing/dai2026/ablations/`: five adaptive-policy conditions and 27,000
  traces.
- `meta-routing/aaai2027/`: executable-task, challenge, ablation, and
  sensitivity artifacts for the AAAI meta-routing paper.
- `oracle2026/`: placeholder for multilingual/cultural reasoning extensions.

Files:

- `tasks.csv`: benchmark task profiles and routing-need annotations
- `traces.csv`: one row per task, policy, and seed execution
- `summary.csv`: aggregate success, cost, latency, and utility
- `comparisons.csv`: paired differences between adaptive and comparison policies
- `details.json`: configuration, workload success, action counts, and failures

COA outcomes are generated by the offline synthetic simulator in
`src/coageneration`. Meta-routing outcomes are generated by the offline
simulator in `src/metarouter/simulator.py` and executable benchmark modules.
They are not measurements from a production deployment or live language model.
